Clearing Machine Blockages & Jams

Safe procedure for isolating power, opening access hatches, and removing material blockages from the sieve boat or aspirator duct.

CRITICAL SAFETY WARNING

DANGER: NEVER insert hands or tools into the machine while power is connected. Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) is mandatory before opening access doors!

  1. Press the red E-STOP button on the control panel or remote station.
  2. Turn main isolator switch to OFF position and secure with personal padlock and lockout tag.
  3. Wait minimum 3 minutes for sieve boat eccentric and blower fan to come to complete standstill.
  4. Unclamp quick-release latches on side inspection doors.
  5. Using wooden scraper or brass scraper (non-sparking), remove accumulated straw, foreign objects, or damp grain crust.
  6. Inspect vertical aspiration channel and clean air distribution vanes.
  7. Close all inspection doors, verify latches are locked, remove LOTO padlock, reset E-STOP, and initiate standard startup.
