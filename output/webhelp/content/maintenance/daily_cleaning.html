Daily Cleaning Procedure

Proper daily cleaning ensures optimal performance and longevity of the GrainFlow X50.

Safety First

Always disconnect the machine from the power supply before cleaning. Use lock-out/tag-out procedures.
  1. Shut down the machine and disconnect power.
  2. Open the access doors and safety guards.
  3. Remove and clean the pre-filter screens.

    Use compressed air to remove debris, or wash with water and mild detergent if heavily soiled.

  4. Clean the interior chambers with a soft brush and vacuum.

    Pay special attention to corners where dust can accumulate.

  5. Wipe down the exterior with a clean, dry cloth.
  6. Close all access doors and guards.
  7. Reconnect power and perform a system check.

Cleaning Schedule

Component Frequency
Pre-filter screens Every 8 hours (or shift change)
Interior chambers Daily
Exterior Daily
Main filter system Weekly
