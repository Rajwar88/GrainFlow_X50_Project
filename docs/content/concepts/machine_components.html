Machine Components Overview

Structural breakdown and key functional assemblies of the GrainFlow X50 grain cleaning machine.

Primary Sub-Assemblies

The GrainFlow X50 consists of five primary mechanical and electrical sub-assemblies engineered for maximum reliability under continuous duty.

1. Feed Hopper & Variable Speed Feed Roller

Accepts raw bulk grain up to 1200 L and ensures an even, single-kernel thick curtain across the entire width of the aspirator channel.

2. Reciprocating Sieve Boat

Constructed from lightweight aircraft-grade aluminum. Holds interchangeable sieve frames equipped with rubber ball cleaners to prevent screen blinding during operation.

3. Air Recirculation Separator & Blower Fan

Driven by a 45 kW motor, generating up to 14,000 m³/h airflow. Recirculates 85% of process air to minimize external dust emissions.

4. Heavy-Duty Cyclone Collector

Separates fine chaff and airborne dust from exhaust air before discharging collected waste into waste bins or central dust extraction systems.

5. Touchscreen Control Panel (Bühler YieldMaster UI)

Provides centralized control over feed rate, air damper position, sieve frequency, and real-time sensor diagnostic readouts.
