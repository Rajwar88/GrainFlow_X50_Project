Medium Severity Operational Cautions

CAUTION: Sieve Frame Bolt Torque

CAUTION:

CAUTION: Do not over-tighten sieve frame clamping bolts beyond 45 Nm. Over-tightening can warp aluminum screen frames and void screen warranty.

CAUTION: Air Ducting Seal Integrity

CAUTION:

CAUTION: Ensure all access panel foam gaskets are free of grit before clamping. Damaged seals reduce aspiration efficiency by up to 25%.
