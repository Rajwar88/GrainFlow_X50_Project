Shutdown Procedure

Follow these steps to safely shut down the GrainFlow X50.

  1. Press the Stop button on the HMI.

    The machine will begin its cooldown cycle (approx 30 seconds).

  2. Wait for the machine to completely stop.

    Do not open any guards until the machine has completely stopped.

  3. Turn the main power switch to the OFF position.
  4. Lock out/tag out the power supply if maintenance is required.

    Always use lock-out/tag-out procedures before performing any maintenance or cleaning.

Post-Shutdown Checklist

  • Record production data from the HMI
  • Note any anomalies observed during operation
  • Clean the machine if required for the next shift
