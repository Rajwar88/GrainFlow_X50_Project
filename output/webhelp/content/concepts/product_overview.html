Product Overview

The GrainFlow X50 is a high-capacity grain cleaning system designed for industrial agricultural operations.

Introduction

The GrainFlow X50 is a state-of-the-art machine that efficiently separates impurities from grains using precision airflow and advanced screening technology. It is part of the GrainFlow Series.

Key Features

  • Throughput: Up to 80 tonnes per hour
  • Efficiency: 99.5% impurity removal rate
  • Energy Consumption: 15% more efficient than previous models
  • IoT Enabled: Remote monitoring via Bühler Connect
  • Safety: Advanced safety interlocks and emergency stop system

Applications

The GrainFlow X50 is suitable for:

  • Wheat
  • Barley
  • Corn/Maize
  • Rice
  • Soybeans
  • Oats

Standard Model

The standard model offers throughput up to 50 tonnes per hour with basic IoT monitoring.

Advanced Model

The advanced model offers throughput up to 80 tonnes per hour with enhanced IoT capabilities including predictive maintenance alerts.
