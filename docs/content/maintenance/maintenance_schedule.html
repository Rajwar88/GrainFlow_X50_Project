Master Maintenance Schedule

Comprehensive preventative maintenance intervals for the GrainFlow X50, categorised by daily, weekly, monthly, and annual service routines.

Daily Maintenance Tasks (Every 8-10 Operating Hours)

  • Inspect feed hopper for foreign objects.
  • Clean aspirator glass windows and light sensors.
  • Check rubber ball cleaning tray underneath sieve screens.
  • Dusty Zone Extra: Blow out motor cooling fan shrouds with compressed air every 4 hours.

Weekly Maintenance Tasks (Every 50 Operating Hours)

  • Inspect sieve deck tension bolts and torque to specified 45 Nm.
  • Check drive belt tension and wear on blower fan pulley.
  • Drain moisture separator on pneumatic control circuit.
  • Cold Zone Extra: Inspect condensate drains daily to prevent frost buildup in pneumatic lines.

Monthly Maintenance Tasks (Every 200 Operating Hours)

  • Grease main eccentric drive shaft bearings with Bühler Food-Grade Synthetic Grease (Buhler-Lube-G2).
  • Inspect rubber flexible isolation mounts for micro-tears or degradation.
  • Check electrical terminal connections inside main control cabinet.

Annual / Major Service (Every 5000 Operating Hours)

  • Replace all sieve boat rubber suspension mounts.
  • Calibrate motor current sensors and vibration monitors.
  • Conduct full laser alignment of drive motor and eccentric shaft.
