Operating Principles

Theory of operation for the GrainFlow X50, explaining aerodynamic aspiration, dual-deck screening, and particle separation mechanics.

Two-Stage Separation Overview

The GrainFlow X50 utilizes a high-efficiency two-stage cleaning process combining high-velocity aerodynamic aspiration with double-deck eccentric screening. Raw grain entering the machine undergoes pre-aspiration before passing over precision-perforated sieve beds, followed by final post-aspiration.

1. Pneumatic Air Separation (Aspiration)

Air separation relies on terminal velocity differences between clean grain kernels and lightweight impurities (straw, chaff, dust, and empty hulls).

  • Pre-Aspiration: As incoming grain cascades over the distribution feed roller, a controlled air curtain extracts light dust prior to mechanical screening.
  • Post-Aspiration: After screening, cleaned grain passes through a vertical air channel with adjustable air velocity matching the specific grain density.

2. Mechanical Sieve Deck Screening

The sieve deck operates via counterbalanced eccentric drives producing a reciprocating throw angle of 12°. Grain flows across two distinct sieve layers:

  • Scalping Sieve (Top Deck): Retains oversized foreign particles (stones, corn cobs, large sticks) while allowing clean grain and fine impurities to drop through.
  • Sand/Fines Sieve (Bottom Deck): Retains prime grain while allowing undersized broken kernels, weed seeds, and sand to fall through the perforations.

Premium Multi-Velocity Air Balance

On GrainFlow X50 Premium models operating at 100 TPH, automated static pressure sensors dynamically regulate the recirculation fan dampers, maintaining optimal separation efficiency across fluctuating moisture levels.
