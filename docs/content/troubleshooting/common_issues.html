Common Issues and Solutions

Quick reference for common operational issues.

Excessive Noise from the Machine

Potential Causes:

  • Loose components or guards
  • Worn bearings
  • Foreign object inside the chamber
  • Belt tension incorrect

Solutions:

  • Stop the machine immediately.
  • Conduct a visual inspection of all external and accessible internal components.
  • Check bearing temperature. Replace if bearings are overheating.
  • If noise persists, contact Bühler Support.

Excessive Vibration During Operation

Potential Causes:

  • Machine not level
  • Imbalanced rotor
  • Worn vibration dampers
  • Uneven feeding

Solutions:

  • Check machine alignment and leveling using a precision level.
  • Adjust leveling feet as needed.
  • Inspect vibration dampers for wear. Replace if cracked or flattened.
  • Ensure even grain flow across the entire width of the machine.

Low Throughput

Potential Causes:

  • Blocked screens or filters
  • Incorrect airflow settings
  • Grain moisture too high
  • Motor speed incorrect

Solutions:

  • Clean screens and filters
  • Adjust airflow settings
  • Dry grain before processing
  • Verify motor RPM settings
