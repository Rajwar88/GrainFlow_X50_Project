First-Time Setup & Commissioning

Step-by-step procedure for unpacking, leveling, electrical connection, and initial calibration of the GrainFlow X50.

Prerequisites

Ensure the installation site satisfies the following requirements:

  • Level concrete foundation rated for minimum 4500 kg static load.
  • Power outlet matching 400V / 50 Hz / 3-phase.
  • Required PPE worn: Safety glasses, hearing protection, safety footwear, dust mask (FFP2).
  1. Unpack & inspect machine for transit damage.

    Remove anti-corrosion transit brackets securing the sieve boat drive eccentric.

  2. Level and mount the machine frame.

    Place spirit level on top frame rail of sieve boat. Adjust corner leveling feet until horizontal deviation is less than 0.5 mm/m. Anchor base plates with M16 expansion bolts.

  3. Connect electrical power supply.

    Connect main power cables to terminal block L1, L2, L3, N, PE in accordance with local regulations. Verify rotation direction of blower motor matches directional arrow.

  4. Perform sensor zero-point calibration.

    From the PLC touchscreen, navigate to Service > Calibration and perform zero-point tare calibration on aspirator air pressure sensors and feed gate actuators.

  5. Execute initial dry test run.

    Perform a 15-minute un-loaded test run to check for abnormal vibration, bearing heat rise, or air seal leakage.
